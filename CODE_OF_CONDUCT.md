# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

为了营造一个开放、友好的社区环境，我们承诺让每一位参与者——无论其背景如何——都能受到尊重，免受骚扰与攻击。

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background.

## 行为规范 / Our Standards

**鼓励的行为 / Positive behavior:**

- 使用友好、包容的语言 / Using welcoming and inclusive language
- 尊重不同观点与经验 / Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- 建设性地反馈，宽容新手 / Giving constructive feedback and showing patience with newcomers

**不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable behavior:**

- 人身攻击、侮辱或骚扰 / Personal attacks, insults, or harassment
- 发布他人的隐私信息 / Publishing others' private information
- 其他专业场合不适用的行为 / Other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

## 执行 / Enforcement

违反本准则的行为可报告给维护者处理，情节严重者将被移除出社区。

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers and may result in removal from the community.
